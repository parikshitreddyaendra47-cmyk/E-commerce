7.Stagging
Step 4 — Important checks before using this in production
This is a first staging implementation, not yet a complete production pipeline.
- Your original checks compare order items against all raw orders. This version deliberately preserves that reference check, so an item referring to an order rejected for a missing customer could still pass the order-reference check. We should decide whether to reject such items too.
- The script assumes quantity and unit_price are numeric. If your CSV columns contain non-numeric values, the comparisons can raise an error. We should inspect their types and define an explicit invalid-value policy.
- Customer and product rows are currently copied into staging without additional row-level rejection rules.
- The script doesn't yet detect duplicate business keys, enforce every documented quality rule, or make repeated runs transactional.
